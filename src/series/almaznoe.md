# Almaznoe

::: {.flatplan_section .flatplan_layout_left_text}

::: {.flatplan_story_text}
The world has shrunk to the boundaries of a neighborhood. A storm of propaganda tears the *Self* apart, confined by a wall of checkpoints at the borders. Yet this neighborhood is still a fractal piece of the entire universe.

This photo project was born as a long journey inward. Through forest, lake, bicycle, seasons, and weather, a person becomes part of the scene, while the scene gazes back. It is a quiet song to the place—a companion on this journey.

With each walk, the collection grows as images intertwine, reintroducing previously discarded shots. The album develops its own shape and internal rhythm, with new mini-series emerging.

Slow down. Free your attention. Take it in. Enter the scene… Rest, look closely at your surroundings, and share your expression with the world.

:::
